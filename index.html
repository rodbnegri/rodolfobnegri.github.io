Hello, I'm Rodolfo.

I am a GNC Researcher & Flight Dynamics Engineer at the Satellite Control Center (CCS) of the National Institute for Space Research (INPE) in Brazil.

My work revolves around astrodynamics, space mission design, nonlinear control, and developing state-of-the-art algorithms for spacecraft autonomy and navigation.

View My Background Read My Publications

About & Experience

I hold a Ph.D. in Space Technology and Engineering from INPE. I am currently leading research in spacecraft autonomy, optical navigation, and GNC algorithms for next-generation missions while overseeing flight dynamics responsibilities over 5 satellites (SCD-1, SCD-2, CBERS-4, CBERS-4A, and AMZ-1).
